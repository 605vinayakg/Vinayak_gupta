[ Core job ]

Pre-homologation

I validated the CO₂ data behind the official fuel economy declaration for 40 to 50 vehicle variants across about 10 models, in a two-person team. For each model I weighed the risk of fuel economy penalties against the cost of over-declaring, and wrote the approval report.

T1

CO₂ analysis tool

25 labs · ~50 users

I owned the logic, ran a proof of concept on 150 to 200 lab and variant combinations, pitched it to the division head and trained every lab on one method.

5,000 ha year · payback ~7 months

T2

Engine data logger

Plug-and-play · 5 sites

Built the business case from 3 years of R&D spend, onboarded the supplier, had the cable built in-house, and trained 10 to 12 QA testers. Still running and being extended.

2,000 ha year · ₹1M+ projected

T3

Data upgrader

Two tools · ~10 users

Spotted missing parameters that would have delayed the logger rollout by 1 to 1.5 years, and designed tools that derive them from raw logged data.

1,100 ha year · payback ~6 months

T4

Template merge

150 → 3 templates

Noticed that 150 variant templates differed mainly by software supplier, and merged them into 3 masters. The work went from a team of 4 to 1.

1,000 ha year · live in 2–3 months

[ Problem solving ]

A CO₂ certification deviation

Traced across 4 labs and 15 to 20 test runs to a test part the procedure didn't call out. The procedure changed in every company lab.

Delay held to ~7 days, with ~100 cars a day of sales at risk.

[ With Suzuki Japan ]

A fault tree before production

Ran a joint fault tree analysis with Suzuki Motor Japan and found a fuel learning problem across vehicles.

Fixed by a software update in ~15 days, before start of production.
